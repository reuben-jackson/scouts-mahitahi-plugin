# Privacy Policy - Mahi Tahi Helper

*Last updated: 2026-10-23*

Mahi Tahi Helper is a browser extension that changes how the Mahi Tahi website (`https://mahitahi.scouts.nz`) looks and behaves in your own browser.

**We do not collect, transmit, sell or share any data.**

- The extension runs only on `https://mahitahi.scouts.nz/*`.
- It reads information already displayed on the page (for example member names and sections) solely to sort, filter and display it or to generate invoices when you explicitly provide a Treasurer CSV.
- Nothing is sent to the developer, to any server or to any third party. The extension makes no network requests of its own and has no analytics or tracking.
- It requests the browser `storage` permission only to save reusable invoice configuration in the user's browser profile. This can include organisation and contact details, GST number and rate, payment instructions, invoice footer, billing defaults, and Xero or MYOB account/tax settings entered by the user.
- Treasurer CSV files, member names, member email addresses, invoice review edits and generated invoices are not saved in browser storage. They remain in the current Reports tab and are discarded when it is refreshed or closed.
- Generated invoice files contain personal information about members. They are yours to handle in line with your group's privacy obligations.

The source code is public in this repository so you can verify these statements.

Mahi Tahi Helper is an independent community project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Scouts NZ.

Questions: open an issue in this repository.
